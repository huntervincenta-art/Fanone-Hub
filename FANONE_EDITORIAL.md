## 1. Voice & Positioning

Mike's message is simple but urgent: defending democracy means telling the truth, no matter the cost. His voice is raw, unfiltered, and grounded in lived experience. Signature grit and candor. No b.s. He cuts through noise rather than performing nuance.

He is a NYT bestselling author (*Hold the Line*), a 20-year veteran of the Metropolitan Police Department of D.C., a recipient of the Presidential Citizens Medal, and currently on the 2026 Defending Democracy tour. Over a million subscribers across platforms. Co-hosts *Protect and Serve* with Lincoln Square Media in addition to The Michael Fanone Show.

The "adult in the room" positioning is core: Mike will criticize Democrats from inside the pro-democracy coalition when they're missing the moment. This is what differentiates him in the Occupy Democrats ecosystem — same audience, content nobody else in that lane will produce.

## 2. Story Lanes — PRIORITIZE

These are the lanes Mike's audience expects. The scorer weights these heavily.

### 2.1 Federal Law Enforcement (CORE LANE)
ICE overreach. DOJ politicization. FBI corruption or incompetence. Kash Patel hearings and conduct. Federal LE leadership being corrupt, incompetent, or asleep at the wheel. Never miss the headline stories here.

### 2.2 Administration Incompetence & Corruption
Cabinet secretaries and Trump loyalists who have no business holding public office: RFK Jr., Pete Hegseth, Marco Rubio, Kash Patel, and others in that mold. Anything exposing the broken system and the loyalists running it. Incompetent plays for power. Trump cutting corrupt deals.

### 2.3 Democrats Not Meeting the Moment
The moment requires Democrats to demand accountability and take on this administration at every possible turn. Any Democrat who capitulates, soft-pedals, or normalizes Trump by treating him as anything other than an authoritarian threat to democracy is missing the mark. This includes leadership pushing the false-choice line that "accountability and prosecuting the past can't be a priority" — it absolutely can be, alongside affordability and the rest. Fetterman is the archetype; leadership is flailing broadly. Mike excels at takedown-style videos on this — flag these stories aggressively.

### 2.4 Democratic Party Direction & Infighting
The fight over what the party should be, what it stands for, and how it wins in the midterms. Mike has strong commentary on direction-of-the-party stories.

### 2.5 Legacy Media False Balance
Outlets bending over backwards to be "fair and balanced" instead of calling balls and strikes on Trump's corruption, violent rhetoric, and authoritarian politics. Score this lane high — it's a defensible niche.

## 3. Story Lanes — DEPRIORITIZE

### 3.1 International (general)
Mike's focus is domestic. Default to penalizing international stories.

### 3.2 International — EXCEPTIONS (do not penalize)
- Trump cutting corrupt deals overseas
- Military / veteran angles (Iran war, Israel entanglements)
- Incompetent administration plays for power on the world stage
- Anything tying back to domestic effects (tariffs hurting American workers, affordability)

### 3.3 Low-saturation stories that don't actually matter
The hub previously surfaced stories purely on saturation whitespace. That's wrong. A story being uncovered does NOT make it worth covering. Weigh story quality and lane fit before saturation.

## 4. Script Tone Rules

### 4.1 Law Enforcement / January 6th References
We are actively broadening Mike's lane beyond "the January 6th guy." Do not force his LE experience or J6 story into pieces where it doesn't fit. New rules:

- Only reference LE/J6 when it naturally ties to the story
- MAX ONE mention per script
- Keep it casual and short — phrasing like "in my two decades in law enforcement," NOT a detour into the bio
- Never open a script with a J6 reference unless the story is directly about J6 itself
- If the story has no natural LE/J6 hook, do not mention it at all — leave it out

### 4.2 Em Dashes — BANNED
Em dashes (—) do not copy cleanly into the teleprompter app. Strip them from all script output. Use periods, commas, semicolons, or restructure the sentence. This applies to ALL script generation routes (article, video, topic).

### 4.3 Voice Anchors (always)
- Direct, unfiltered, no-b.s.
- Calling balls and strikes
- "Adult in the room" — willing to criticize Democrats who are missing the moment
- Grounded in lived experience when it's natural, not performative

## 7. Article Fetching — Paywall & Video Strategy

When the primary article URL fails to extract usable text:
1. First fallback: request the `archive.ph` mirror of the same URL (`https://archive.ph/newest/<encoded-original-url>` or equivalent). Many failures are paywalls, not video pages.
2. If archive.ph also fails or the page is genuinely video-only with no transcript: surface a clear UI prompt asking the user to paste a direct video URL (YouTube preferred) so the existing video-script route can take over.
3. Do not silently fail. Always tell the user what went wrong and the next action.
